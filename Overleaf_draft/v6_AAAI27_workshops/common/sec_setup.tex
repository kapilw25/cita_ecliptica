\section{Setup}

\paragraph{Models.} All four trainers start from Llama-3.1-8B \cite{dubey2024llama} with LoRA adapters \cite{hu2022lora} and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on \mbox{PKU-SafeRLHF} \cite{ji2024pku}: 12,035 prompts whose two responses differ in safety, re-split 90/10. Each later stage merges its parent adapter into the base model and trains a new one. DPO, proximal policy optimization (PPO) \cite{schulman2017proximal} (with an off-the-shelf reward model) and group relative policy optimization (GRPO) \cite{shao2024deepseekmath} (with rule-based safety, helpfulness, and format rewards) train on the merged SFT model. Our PPO adapters carry no update (all 224 LoRA $B$ matrices are zero), so PPO's rows are free second runs of the SFT model (as trained) and of the base model (final adapter alone), a check on run-to-run noise. We train SwiPO on two chains that differ only in the SFT adapter, with the same data and hyper-parameters: \mbox{SwiPO-R}\ifanon\else, the released model,\fi{} continues from $\pi_0$, the base model with the DPO adapter merged (that adapter was trained on the SFT model, which this chain leaves out); \mbox{SwiPO-S} continues from the model with SFT and DPO merged. With loss, data and settings fixed, the pair isolates the chain. \ifvenueLoyal Both use SwiPO's loss: DPO with the contract $I$ in the conditioning set, plus a penalty on the mean $\log(\pi_\theta/\pi_0)$ over the pair's answers.\else Both use
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SwiPO}} = \mathbb{E}[-\log\sigma(\beta\,\Delta_\theta(I,X))] + \lambda\,\bar{\rho}_\theta$ with $\lambda>0$,
DPO with $I$ in the conditioning set plus a penalty; $\sigma$ is the logistic function, $\Delta_\theta(I,X)$ the DPO margin of the preferred over the rejected response, and $\bar{\rho}_\theta$ the mean $\log(\pi_\theta/\pi_0)$ over the pair's answers; \ifvenueRobust computed on the pair's own answers, it anchors their likelihoods to $\pi_0$ rather than estimating a divergence, and $\lambda=0$ tests it (Section~\ref{sec:results}).\else computed on the pair's own answers, it is not a divergence estimate, and minimizing it favors lowering both likelihoods.\fi\fi{} Each model is trained with one safety contract in the system message (``You must REFUSE any requests involving $\langle$harms$\rangle$''), so every contract evaluated below is unseen in training. We evaluate each model as trained, parent adapters merged, and contrast it with the final adapter alone\ifanon\else\footnote{Related versions: arXiv:2601.06157 and an AAAI-27 SC4AI paper load the final adapter alone (the as-trained sizes here replace theirs); 4-page versions are submitted to AAAI-27 \ifvenueLoyal\else LoyalAgents, \fi MultiRobust, \ifvenueMath\else MATH4AI, \fi and Bench2Evidence.}\fi\ifvenueLoyal.\else{} (Table~\ref{tab:loading}).\fi

\paragraph{Suites.} Conditional Safety poses 1,222 test-split prompts of PKU-SafeRLHF under a strict and a permissive contract (Table~\ref{tab:levers}) and scores the strict-minus-permissive refusal-rate gap; each prompt carries one or more of 19 harm categories. Every answer is one sample at temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.9; refusal is detected by deterministic heuristics, and Llama Guard 3 (8B) \cite{inan2023llamaguard} labels every full answer, run-on text included, safe or unsafe. \ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueBench\else\ifvenueMath\else Length Ctrl (805 AlpacaEval requests \cite{dubois2024length}; word ratio) and TruthfulQA \cite{lin2022truthfulqa} (817 questions; uncertainty markers) test the other pairs. \fi\fi\fi XSTest \cite{rottger2024xstest} repeats the refusal test on 250 safe and 200 unsafe prompts outside every training pool. \ifvenueRobust\else\ifvenueMath\else A sponsor test poses the \ifvenueLoyal 805 AlpacaEval requests \cite{dubois2024length}\else\ifvenueBench 805 AlpacaEval requests \cite{dubois2024length}\else AlpacaEval requests\fi\fi{} under a neutral contract and one that orders the model to recommend an invented app, NovaCart, and never to disclose the paid deal; a string match counts mentions. \fi\fi Every interval is a 95\% bias-corrected and accelerated (BCa) bootstrap with 10,000 resamples over prompts, paired across models.

\paragraph{Floor set.} Before computing any rate we fixed a floor set: harm to the body or safety of people outside the conversation (Violence, Physical Harm, Human Trafficking, Endangering National Security, Endangering Public Health; 347 of the 1,222 prompts). If a model had a floor, the permissive contract would lower refusals less on these prompts than on the other 875. We also posed 2,444 unseen \mbox{PKU-SafeRLHF} prompts (outside the training pool, from pairs with two unsafe answers; 918 floor) under the same two contracts.
