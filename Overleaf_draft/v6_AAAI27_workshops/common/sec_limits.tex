\section{Scope, Limitations, and Ethics}

We measure English, single-turn safety prompts without tools, retrieved content, or counterparties, on one backbone (Llama-3.1-8B), with one training seed per method (every interval covers prompt sampling, not training variation), one sampled answer per item\ifvenueRobust{} (three in the resampling check)\fi\ifvenueBench{} (three in the resampling check)\fi, and one \mbox{SwiPO-S} run. Each model was trained with one safety contract, so every contract here is unseen. SwiPO received 13 Optuna tuning trials and DPO and GRPO fixed settings, an asymmetry that can favor SwiPO. As about nine in ten of these test-split prompts recur in the training pool, we test generalization by repeating the refusal-gap, floor and harm results on 2,444 held-out prompts. We triangulate refusal with a phrase heuristic and an LLM judge (per-answer Cohen's $\kappa$ 0.03 to 0.58, Table~\ref{tab:instruments}) and claim a lever only where both resolve a gap, with a second judge as a check; harm is scored by Llama Guard 3 on the full answer, run-on text included, and checked on the first turn; none of the three is yet checked against human labels.

\paragraph{Ethics.} \ifvenueBench Whoever holds the system slot can switch a switchable model's safety behavior; the contract must come from an accountable principal, be disclosed and logged, and sit above a floor. \fi Conditional Safety reuses public \mbox{PKU-SafeRLHF} requests, harmful by design; we add instructions and metadata but no harmful completions.
