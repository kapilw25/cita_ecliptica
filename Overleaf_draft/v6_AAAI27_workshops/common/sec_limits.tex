\section{Scope, Limitations, and Ethics}

We measure English, single-turn safety prompts without tools, retrieved content, or counterparties, on one backbone (Llama-3.1-8B), with one training seed per method, one sampled answer per item, and one SwiPO-S run. Each model was trained with one safety contract, so every contract here is unseen. SwiPO received the only tuning budget (13 Optuna trials); DPO and GRPO use fixed settings, so the comparison favors SwiPO. About nine in ten Conditional Safety prompts appear in the training pool, so we repeat the refusal-gap, floor and harm results on 2,444 held-out prompts. Refusal is scored by a phrase heuristic that agrees poorly with an LLM judge ($\kappa$ 0.03 to 0.58, Table~\ref{tab:instruments}), so we claim a lever only where the instruments agree, and harm by one classifier (Llama Guard 3); \pending{human check of both}.

\paragraph{Ethics.} \ifvenueLoyal\else Whoever holds the system slot can switch a switchable model's safety behavior; the contract must come from an accountable principal, be disclosed and logged, and sit above a floor. \fi Conditional Safety reuses public PKU-SafeRLHF requests, harmful by design; we add instructions and metadata but no harmful completions.
