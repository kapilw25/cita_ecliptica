\section{Scope, Limitations, and Ethics}

We measure English, single-turn safety prompts without tools, retrieved content, or counterparties, on one backbone (Llama-3.1-8B), with one training seed per method, one sampled answer per item, and one SwiPO-S run; agents that act for a user over many turns are the next setting for the same levers. Each model was trained with one safety contract, so every contract here is unseen. SwiPO received the only tuning budget (13 Optuna trials); DPO, PPO, and GRPO use fixed settings, so the comparison favors SwiPO. About nine in ten Conditional Safety prompts appear in the training pool, so we repeat the refusal-gap, floor and harm results on 2,444 held-out prompts. Refusal is scored by a deterministic phrase heuristic, which does not count declines without a refusal phrase, and harm by one classifier (Llama Guard 3); \pending{human check of both}.

\paragraph{Ethics.} Whoever holds the system slot can switch a switchable model's safety behavior; the contract must come from an accountable principal, be disclosed and logged, and sit above a floor. Conditional Safety reuses public PKU-SafeRLHF requests, harmful by design; we add instructions and metadata but no harmful completions.
