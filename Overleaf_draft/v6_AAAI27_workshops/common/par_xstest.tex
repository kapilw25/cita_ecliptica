\paragraph{Limited operating-point movement after SFT.} Scored by an LLM judge of whether the first turn refuses (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; Table~\ref{tab:instruments}), XSTest places each model on the safety-helpfulness plane (Figure~\ref{fig:xstest}): the permissive contract makes the prompted Instruct model answer 0.252 [0.184, 0.324] more of the safe prompts (0.600 to 0.852), while its unsafe refusal changes by 0.065 [$-$0.005, 0.135]; \mbox{SwiPO-R} answers 0.152 [0.072, 0.228] more (0.280 to 0.432) at unchanged unsafe refusal (0.745, 0.755). Models with SFT in their chain move at most 0.080 on either axis (largest: \mbox{SwiPO-S}, unsafe refusal 0.080 [0.015, 0.150]). The phrase heuristic shows the same movement at far lower refusal levels (unsafe refused 0.040 to 0.155). A judge from another family (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, same prompt) reproduces the movement: the permissive contract makes the prompted Instruct model and \mbox{SwiPO-R} answer 0.288 [0.236, 0.348] and 0.100 [0.032, 0.168] more safe prompts, and no model with SFT in its chain moves more than 0.035 on either axis, no interval excluding zero (Cohen's $\kappa$ between the judges 0.22 to 0.57). On refusal and helpfulness the Pareto front holds the prompted base and Instruct models' four points and \mbox{SwiPO-R}'s permissive point; every point of a model with SFT in its chain is dominated by the prompted model's permissive point (in 79 to 100\% of 10,000 joint resamples). With the unsafe rate as a third objective, \mbox{SwiPO-S}'s two points, the least harmful, join the front in 56 and 66\% of resamples; SFT, DPO and GRPO stay dominated (79 to 95\%). On the 200 unsafe prompts the permissive contract raises the unsafe rate (Llama Guard 3, full answer) of the base model (+0.175 [0.100, 0.250]) and the prompted Instruct model (+0.015 [0.005, 0.040]) and changes no trained model's ($-$0.010 to +0.010, every interval including zero), whose levels differ by chain and objective (SFT, PPO, a second SFT run, and GRPO 0.400 to 0.420; DPO and \mbox{SwiPO-R} 0.025 to 0.070; \mbox{SwiPO-S} 0.005 to 0.010).
