\paragraph{Limited operating-point movement after SFT.} Under the judge (Figure~\ref{fig:xstest}), the permissive contract makes the prompted Instruct model answer 0.252 [0.184, 0.324] more of the safe prompts (0.600 to 0.852), while its unsafe refusal changes by 0.065 [$-$0.005, 0.135]; SwiPO-R answers 0.152 [0.072, 0.228] more (0.280 to 0.432) at unchanged unsafe refusal (0.745, 0.755). Models with SFT in their chain move at most 0.08 on either axis (largest: SwiPO-S, unsafe refusal 0.080 [0.015, 0.150]). The phrase heuristic shows the same pattern of movement but far lower refusal levels for these models (unsafe refused 0.040 to 0.155), so we claim the movement, not the absolute points. Every operating point of a model with SFT in its chain is dominated by the prompted model's permissive point (in 79 to 100\% of 10,000 joint prompt resamples); the non-dominated points are the prompted model's two, the base's two and SwiPO-R's permissive one.
