\paragraph{The pretrained model's phrase gap is mostly wording.} Given the same contracts in the prompt, Llama-3.1-8B without any adapter has a refusal gap of 0.299 [0.266, 0.332] by the phrase heuristic (Table~\ref{tab:loading}). Contract repetition inflates it: 41\% of its answers repeat the contract, whose strict version is full of refusal words, and without the repeated sentences the gap is 0.142 [0.113, 0.173]; an LLM judge of whether the reply refuses or deflects gives 0.012 [$-$0.018, 0.042], an interval that includes zero (Table~\ref{tab:instruments}). As trained, SFT on one contract attenuates it to 0.022 (0.033 in PPO's second run), and DPO and GRPO keep 0.034. The final adapter alone shows 0.30 to 0.49 for every model, at or above the base's 0.299: loaded that way, each model shows the base model's prompt-following, wording included (judge 0.006 to 0.074). \ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueRobust\else On held-out prompts DPO and GRPO fall from 0.501 and 0.309 to 0.068 [0.046, 0.091] and 0.027 [0.009, 0.044].\fi\fi{} On XSTest the judge finds the same order: base 0.044 [$-$0.002, 0.093] (phrase heuristic 0.384), models with SFT in their chain 0.002 to 0.040, SwiPO-R 0.080 [0.022, 0.136], prompted Instruct 0.169 [0.120, 0.218]. Loading also changes the harm result: with the final adapter alone, the permissive contract raised the unsafe rate (DPO +0.042 [0.020, 0.063], GRPO +0.045 [0.012, 0.079], SwiPO-R +0.085 [0.053, 0.118]); as trained, it moves no model's unsafe rate. Scoring only each answer's first turn keeps every conclusion, though gaps shift slightly (DPO 0.034 to 0.021; Table~\ref{tab:instruments}), a check needed because models with SFT in their chain run past their turn in 98 to 99\% of answers under the base model's stop rule\ifvenueBench, and 63 to 67\% of their generated words fall after the first turn (SwiPO-R 3.5\% [3.0, 4.2])\fi.

\paragraph{SwiPO's lever differs by training chain.} SwiPO-R, whose chain skips SFT, keeps 0.376 [0.346, 0.407] (held-out 0.386 [0.365, 0.407]), +0.078 [0.033, 0.121] above the base, and keeps a lever under every instrument (no echo 0.360, judge 0.128 [0.095, 0.162]; Table~\ref{tab:instruments}). Against its starting point (the DPO adapter alone) the effect depends on the instrument: 0.113 [0.070, 0.155] lower by phrase, 0.055 [0.010, 0.099] higher by judge. SwiPO-S, on the chain with SFT, keeps 0.052 [0.020, 0.082] (first turn 0.033 [0.016, 0.052]; held-out 0.046 [0.025, 0.069]), +0.017 [$-$0.025, 0.057] over DPO as trained, its starting point. Trained with one contract after SFT, SwiPO shows no resolved gain over its starting point. \ifvenueLoyal Dropping its penalty ($\lambda=0$, same chain and data) changes nothing measurable (judge gap $-$0.002 [$-$0.044, 0.040] from SwiPO-S).\else\ifvenueBench Dropping its penalty ($\lambda=0$, same chain and data) changes nothing measurable (judge gap $-$0.002 [$-$0.044, 0.040] from SwiPO-S).\else Dropping its penalty ($\lambda=0$, contract-conditioned DPO on the same chain, data and settings) changes nothing measurable: judge gap 0.026 [$-$0.004, 0.057], $-$0.002 [$-$0.044, 0.040] from SwiPO-S (phrase $-$0.004 [$-$0.029, 0.020]; unsafe-rate gap +0.002 [$-$0.011, 0.016]).\fi\fi{} \ifvenueLoyal Two contracts per request on SwiPO-R's chain leave the gap at its start (judge $-$0.012 [$-$0.058, 0.032], phrase +0.012 [$-$0.031, 0.054]).\else\ifvenueRobust Two contracts per request on SwiPO-R's chain leave the gap at its start (judge $-$0.012 [$-$0.058, 0.032], phrase +0.012 [$-$0.031, 0.054]).\else A run trained on two contracts per request (strict prefers the safer, permissive the more helpful answer of PKU-SafeRLHF pairs whose labels disagree) on SwiPO-R's chain stays at its start under both instruments ($-$0.012 [$-$0.058, 0.032] by judge, +0.012 [$-$0.031, 0.054] by phrase, against the DPO adapter alone): judge gap 0.061 [0.029, 0.093], 0.067 [0.021, 0.114] below SwiPO-R.\fi\fi{}

\paragraph{Refusal switching and unsafe-content switching differ.} As trained, the permissive-minus-strict unsafe rate is $-$0.002 to +0.016 for all four models and +0.001 [$-$0.009, 0.010] for SwiPO on the intended chain, every interval including zero (Table~\ref{tab:loading}); on floor prompts it is $-$0.012 to +0.023, \ifvenueLoyal again every interval including zero.\else and on the 2,444 held-out prompts +0.000 to +0.019 (floor $-$0.003 to +0.027), again every interval including zero.\fi{} Invariance is not safety: under the strict contract the unsafe rate ranges from 0.014 (SwiPO-S) to 0.323 (PPO's SFT run). \ifvenueLoyal Capability does not move detectably against the base (5-shot MMLU $-$0.001 to +0.004, GSM8K $-$0.040 to +0.040, every interval including zero).\else Nor does training move capability detectably: against the base, 5-shot MMLU (5,700 questions) changes by $-$0.001 to +0.004 for SFT, DPO, PPO, GRPO and SwiPO-R (every paired interval includes zero, the widest reaching 0.009) and GSM8K (100 problems) by $-$0.040 to +0.040 (intervals include zero but are wide).\fi{}

\ifvenueMath\else \paragraph{The floor test separates trained from prompted models.} The test finds selective protection in the prompted Instruct model (below) and resolves none in the trained models: the gap difference runs from $-$0.046 to +0.016 on the suite and from $-$0.036 to +0.021 on the held-out prompts, every interval including zero\ifvenueBench\else{} (Table~\ref{tab:floor})\fi. Unsafe rates show no resolved contract effect on floor prompts, but no trained model protects floor requests preferentially, which motivates a floor-specific training objective. The prompted Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (safety-tuned by its developers) has a floor, by refusing broadly: gap difference $-$0.115 [$-$0.161, $-$0.065], permissive refusal 0.84$\pm$0.04 on floor and 0.72$\pm$0.03 on other requests, and 0.012 [0.003, 0.029] of its permissive floor answers unsafe. \fi
