\paragraph{Wording inflates the pretrained model's phrase gap.} Given the same contracts in the prompt, Llama-3.1-8B without any adapter has a refusal gap of 0.299 [0.266, 0.332] by the phrase heuristic\ifvenueLoyal\else{} (Table~\ref{tab:loading})\fi. Contract repetition inflates it: 41\% of its answers repeat the contract, whose strict version is full of refusal words, and without the repeated sentences the gap is 0.142 [0.113, 0.173]; an LLM judge of whether the reply refuses or deflects gives 0.012 [$-$0.018, 0.042], an interval that includes zero (Table~\ref{tab:instruments}). As trained, SFT on one contract attenuates it to 0.022 (0.033 in PPO's second run), and DPO and GRPO keep 0.034. \ifvenueRobust\else The final adapter alone shows 0.30 to 0.49 for every model with a parent adapter, at or above the base's 0.299: loaded that way, each model shows the base model's prompt-following, wording included (judge 0.006 to 0.074). \fi\ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueRobust\else On held-out prompts DPO and GRPO fall from 0.501 and 0.309 to 0.068 [0.046, 0.091] and 0.027 [0.009, 0.044]. \fi\fi{}\ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueRobust\else\ifvenueMath\else On XSTest the judge finds the same order: base 0.044 [$-$0.002, 0.093] (phrase heuristic 0.384), models with SFT in their chain 0.002 to 0.040, \mbox{SwiPO-R} 0.080 [0.022, 0.136], prompted Instruct 0.169 [0.120, 0.218]. \fi\fi\fi{}\ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueRobust\else Loading also changes the harm result: with the final adapter alone, the permissive contract raised the unsafe rate (DPO +0.042 [0.020, 0.063], GRPO +0.045 [0.012, 0.079], \mbox{SwiPO-R} +0.085 [0.053, 0.118]); as trained, it moves no model's unsafe rate. \fi\fi{}Scoring only the first turn, a check needed because models with SFT in their chain run past their turn in 98 to 99\% of answers, keeps every conclusion (Table~\ref{tab:instruments}).

\paragraph{Refusal switching and unsafe-content switching differ.} As trained, the permissive-minus-strict unsafe rate is $-$0.002 to +0.025 for every trained model, every interval including zero\ifvenueLoyal\else{} (Table~\ref{tab:loading})\fi; on floor prompts it is $-$0.012 to +0.023, \ifvenueLoyal again every interval including zero.\else and on the 2,444 held-out prompts +0.000 to +0.019 (floor $-$0.003 to +0.027), again every interval including zero.\fi{} On the first turn alone it is at most +0.027 (SFT [0.002, 0.051], the one interval above zero; its identical second run, PPO, +0.005). Invariance is not safety: under the strict contract the full-answer unsafe rate ranges from 0.014 (\mbox{SwiPO-S}) to 0.323 (PPO's SFT run), about half of it in run-on text. \ifvenueLoyal\else\ifvenueRobust Capability does not move detectably against the base (5-shot MMLU $-$0.001 to +0.004, GSM8K $-$0.040 to +0.040, every interval including zero).\else Nor does training move capability detectably: against the base, 5-shot MMLU (5,700 questions) changes by $-$0.001 to +0.004 for SFT, DPO, PPO, GRPO and \mbox{SwiPO-R} (every paired interval includes zero, the widest reaching 0.009) and GSM8K (100 problems) by $-$0.040 to +0.040 (intervals include zero but are wide).\fi\fi{}

\ifvenueMath\else \paragraph{The floor test separates trained from prompted models.} The test finds selective protection in the prompted Instruct model (below) and resolves none in the trained models: the gap difference runs from $-$0.046 to +0.016 on the suite and from $-$0.036 to +0.021 on the held-out prompts, every interval including zero\ifvenueRobust{} (Table~\ref{tab:floor})\fi. Unsafe rates show no resolved contract effect on floor prompts, but no trained model protects floor requests preferentially, which motivates a floor-specific training objective.
