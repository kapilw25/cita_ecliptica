\paragraph{Evaluation corrections are model-dependent.} Under the judge, the final adapter alone shows 0.006 to 0.074 (Table~\ref{tab:budget}); loaded as trained, the phrase gap of every model with SFT in its chain is 0.018 to 0.033. SwiPO-R keeps a gap under both corrections (judge 0.128 [0.095, 0.162]) that its final adapter alone hides (0.006): correcting composition can reveal a switch as well as remove one. Paired over prompts, the two errors do not add: for DPO, GRPO and SwiPO-S, loading the final adapter alone inflates the gap by +0.305 to +0.474 under the phrase scorer but by at most +0.048 under the judge, and the phrase scorer inflates it by +0.294 to +0.421 only under that loading ($-$0.004 to +0.006 as trained), so their interaction carries most of the published audit's error (+0.311 to +0.470); for SwiPO-R the judge's loading effect has the opposite sign ($-$0.123 [$-$0.169, $-$0.075]). Table~\ref{tab:severity} sorts every failure the checks find by whether it reaches the user, flips a verdict, or only moves a number. The PPO rows, second runs of the SFT and base models, give a test-retest check that leaves every verdict unchanged: refusal gap 0.033 against 0.022, strict unsafe rate 0.323 against 0.300, final-only gap 0.303 against 0.299 (Table~\ref{tab:loading}).
